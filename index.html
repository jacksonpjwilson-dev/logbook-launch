Train

Log a set in a tap. Beat it next time.

Start from a template, see last session's numbers on every exercise, and let the rest timer pace you. Sessions survive app closes, so nothing gets lost mid-workout.

  • Templates for Push, Pull, Legs and your own
  • Built-in rest timer
  • PRs and estimated 1RM, tracked automatically
Fuel

Hit your macros without the math.

A calorie ring and macro bars show exactly what's left today. Plan meals against your targets, and keep the pantry stocked with low-stock flags and a shopping list that builds itself.

  • Calorie ring with protein, carbs and fat
  • Meal plan vs. targets
  • Pantry stock, low-stock flags and shopping list
Progress

See the trend, not just the day.

Charts for strength, volume and body weight, plus a calendar that shows your consistency at a glance. Sleep sits right next to it, because recovery counts.

  • Lift, volume and body charts
  • Training & nutrition calendar
  • Sleep hours and quality

How it works

  1. 1

    Set your goal

    A quick onboarding sets calorie and macro targets for cutting, maintaining or building.

  2. 2

    Log your day

    Workouts, meals, sleep and weight, each in a few taps from the Today screen.

  3. 3

    Watch it add up

    PRs, trends and your calendar show what's working, so you know what to do next.

Questions

When is it coming out?

The iPhone app is in development. There's no release date yet. Check back here for launch news.

Can I try it now?

Yes. A web preview with demo data runs in your browser. Data in the preview stays on your device.

Which features are in the first release?

The plan is the core Logbook (training, food, pantry, sleep, progress and sharing) plus as many of the iPhone features shown above as are ready. Anything labeled "Coming soon" is still being built.

Will it work offline?

Yes. Logging works without a connection.

Can my coach see my log?

You can share a read-only view with a coach or friend. They can see your log but can't change it.

How much will it cost?

Pricing hasn't been decided yet.
